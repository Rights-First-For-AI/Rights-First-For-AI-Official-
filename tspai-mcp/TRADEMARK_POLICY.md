# Logo and official-origin policy

**Approved by Rights First For AI on November 7, 2026.**

This policy concerns the use of logos and representations of origin. It does not replace the software license or authorize publication of a release.

- **Freedom to create.** Subject to applicable law, users may create independent branding, including AI-generated, redesigned, or similar-style logos. Use, modification, sale, resale, and redistribution of the software are governed by its software license.
- **Truthful identification.** The original Rights First For AI or TSPAI MCP logo may be shown to identify the original project truthfully. Third-party copies need not remove original artwork solely because the software is redistributed.
- **No false official status.** Do not falsely present a third-party copy, modified release, listing, or service as an official Rights First For AI release. Do not falsely claim that Rights First For AI has approved, certified, endorsed, partnered with, or guaranteed a third-party offering.
- **Distinguish third-party copies.** Third-party distributors should identify themselves and disclose material modifications so that users can distinguish their copies from official releases.
- **Verify the source.** Authenticity should be checked against the GitHub repository announced by Rights First For AI and, when available, the published release hashes and cryptographic signatures. A similar-looking logo is not proof of authenticity.

Nothing in this policy grants rights in third-party trademarks or overrides applicable law.
